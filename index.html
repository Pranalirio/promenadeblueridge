The Promenade Residences at Blue Ridge

Set along the riverside promenade, this address claims the most consequential position within Blue Ridge – open to the river, aligned with the sky. Rising thirty-two storeys and standing over 100 metres tall, The Promenade Residences is positioned to look beyond the reach of many.

With only three residences per floor and distinct living and dining areas, life here is defined by privacy, privilege and presence. On one side, the Mula river stretches across an uninterrupted eleven kilometres; on the other, the city reveals itself in scale and movement.

  • Every residence gets its own riverfront deck
  • Separate private resident arrival, away from the retail promenade
  • Exclusive guest entry & walk-in wardrobes
  • Inside an award-winning, fully operational township

Details that define each residence

Flooring

Kitchen

Modular kitchen with chimney, piped gas provision and single-bowl stainless steel sink. Quartz stone platform (4 BHK) / full-body vitrified tile platform (3 BHK). Dado up to 2 ft above platform.

Toilets & Dado

Electrical & Plumbing
